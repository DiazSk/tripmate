✳Method

How it actually works

Four steps, about five minutes. Most of it is the plan being checked rather than written.

01

You set the constraints

Where, when, how much, and who is going. The budget is a target to hit, not a ceiling to stay under.

02

Real data goes in first

Forecast for your actual dates, public holidays, opening hours and travel times — fetched before a single word is written.

03

Written, then critiqued

A second pass reviews the finished plan against your budget and pace, and rewrites the days it finds fault with.

04

Every place is checked

Coordinates are corrected against OpenStreetMap, so the pins land where the place actually is rather than where the model guessed.
