# Model call speed and cost, CLI transport, 2026-11-02

Everything here is the `cli` transport (the default). The paid `api` transport was not run. Model
names are unchanged, and `MODEL` stays `claude-sonnet-4-5`.

## What shipped

| Lever | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_NONESSENTIAL_TRAFFIC=1` on every CLI child | `runClaudeViaCli`, `claude.ts` | Before, 87 of 88 recent traces also billed a call to `claude-haiku-5-5` that the CLI makes on its own (about 2.4k input and 250 output tokens, with thinking). `LLM_CLI_THINKING=type=n,...` overrides the table per call type without a code change. An empty
value (`story=`) restores the CLI default.

A real generation, which is 20 runs with both generate and critique OK, takes **478s** end to end
at p50: about 14s of setup (context and sketch), 192s generating, a steady **~47s of non-model
work** (placing stops, routing legs), then 163s of critique. Thinking is 59 to 80% of generate's
output and 54 to 77% of critique's.

`--effort low` does nothing on this path. The CLI accepts it on sonnet-4-5 and haiku-4-5, and
story thought *more* with it (5,677 and 8,095 tokens, against 4,147 and 7,812 without it). That
includes the `effort: "low"` chat already passes.

## Accuracy evidence

- **Story.** I scored each script against the prompt's own rules: beat count and order, at most 55
  words, 2 to 4 sentences, a stop beat's first sentence at most 8 words, no digits or forbidden
  punctuation, no two "You" openings in a row. Across 3 days and 13 scripts per arm, the rule breaks
  per script were 0.15 at the default, **0.31 at 2,048**, 1.15 at 1,024 and 1.75 with thinking off.
  All arms parsed. The extra breaks at 2,048 were one 9-word first sentence and one stray
  punctuation mark.
- **Meri answer.** On the trip-chat prompt, thinking-off put a place *name* where an id belongs once
  in 3 runs. The default did it 0 times in 3, and a 1,024 budget did it 2 times in 4. So the gap was
  in the prompt, not the thinking. With the ids line added, thinking-off cited ids 6 times in 6, with
  the same months and sources as the default. On Home's prompt, which already had the line, months
  and sources matched across 3 runs per arm. `parseMeriAnswer` and `groundAnswer` are unchanged, and
  their tests pass. A name the evidence does not hold still replaces the answer with "nothing
  checked". In live curl runs against the dev server, all 3 trip-chat lookups answered with places
  taken from evidence ids.
- **Suite.** `npm test` passes 1,868 of 1,868. `tsc --noEmit` is clean after `next typegen`.
  `npm run lint` shows 0 errors (1 old warning in `travelerProfile.test.mjs`).

## Measured but not shipped

- **Generate + critique with no thinking: your decision.** I ran 3 fixtures twice per arm through
  `/api/itinerary` (critique included) and scored the final plan with the bench scorers:

  | | composite mean (range) | wall p50 | generate p50 | critique p50 | critique revised |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | default | 0.894 (0.79-0.97) | 514s | 253s | 195s | 6/6 |
  | thinking 0 | 0.878 (0.77-0.93) | **173s** | **55s** | **46s** | 6/6 |

  Three times faster, and the composite difference is smaller than the spread inside either arm. But
  this is only n=6, the Barcelona plans came back with fewer stops (15-16 against 19-21), and
  `weatherFeasibility` fell on 3 of 6 runs. That is not enough to call quality held, so the default
  is unchanged. To try it: `LLM_CLI_THINKING=generate=0,critique=0`. A budget between 0 and the
  default (for example 4,096) was not measured. These runs had 6 generations in flight at once,
  which is why the baseline generate here is slower than production's 192s.
- **Chat with no thinking: rejected.** Edits ran in 4 to 23s against 24 to 134s, but 4 of 9 refine
  cells came back as prose with a `lookup` instead of a patch ("I need to look up…"), so they
  scored as failures. The default passed 9 of 9.
- **Idea-read with no thinking: rejected.** It is 5.3s against 11.4s, and the field-by-field scores
  were close (65-67 of 70 against 69 of 70). But it wrote the landmark as the question's place
  ("Treasury, Petra, Jordan") 4 times in 4, the geocoder cannot read that, and Home then answers
  nothing. A 1,024 budget bought no speed (8.4s against 8.7s).
- **Place-detail and context with no thinking: not adopted.** They are 10 to 13s against 13 to 27s.
  Both recall facts from model memory with nothing to check them against, and the thinking-off
  place card asserted new specifics ("cash only", "opened in 1900") that I could not verify.
- **Replacing the CLI system prompt** (`--system-prompt`) cut input from 9.3k to 3.1k tokens, but
  latency did not change (2.12s against 2.18s p50, 5 pairs). It would also change how the model
  behaves on every call type, and checking that would mean re-running everything above, so it was
  dropped.

## Follow-ups

- **Landmark questions on Home.** Today only 8 of 16 landmark questions ("near the Eiffel Tower")
  produce a place the geocoder can read. A one-line prompt change ("a landmark inside it is not a
  place") measured 12 of 16 with thinking still on. It is a quality fix rather than a speed one, so
  it is not on this branch.
- **The ~47s between generate and critique** is placing stops and routing legs, not a model call.
- **Lower budgets for generate and critique**, on more fixtures, if you want the 3x. This uses
  subscription capacity: each arm is about 12 full generations.

## Method

I took the prompts from `llm_traces` (newest 3 per type) and replayed them through the CLI with the
same flags `runClaudeViaCli` uses, varying only `MAX_THINKING_TOKENS`. Times are the CLI envelope's
`duration_ms` unless marked wall. Arms ran concurrently, so absolute numbers carry load. Compare
within a table, not across tables. The generation A/B used a dev server on a copy of the database
(`DB_PATH`), with `scripts/critique-ab.mjs` and `/api/bench` refine cells.
